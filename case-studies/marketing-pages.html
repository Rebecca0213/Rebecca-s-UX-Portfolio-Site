Overview

Questwealth Portfolios (QWP) is a product under Questrade (Investing) that offers its customers managed investing services — ETF portfolios handpicked by financial experts/advisors. The purpose of the QWP product landing page is to get more existing customers and new prospects on the website to convert and open a QWP/managed investing account with us.

In this project to redesign the QWP product page, I’m responsible for researching why our QWP Product page has an astounding 80% bounce rate (and a low conversion rate) and to come up with a design optimization solution, especially when the robo-advice market is bound to see a massive market size growth in the next 10 years.

After the new design for the page was pushed live in December, the bounce rate for the QWP landing page saw a drastic decrease — it went from a consistent ~80% for many months (since Jan 2023 and prior) to a bounce rate of ~30% for multiple months after its deployment.

To make for a fair comparison, I set the 2 date ranges to be a period of 6 months long each (i.e. May 1 – Oct 31, 2023 and Nov 1 – Apr 21, 2024) with the sessions being rather similar (around ~500k each during the time period). Though there is a dip in bounce rate during December, it is also important to note that there were also less sessions/users visiting the landing page during the holiday season — but for the months after, we’re seeing a consistent much lower bounce rate compared to before when we had the old landing page.

Design Recommendations

Two frameworks, one structure

We treated the QWP page as a journey in itself — top to bottom mirrors awareness to conversion — then applied the Laws of UX to decide how each zone should be built.

Hick’s Law

More choices, more time to decide — cut CTAs down to one primary + minimal secondary.

Serial Position Effect

First and last items are remembered best — least critical content belongs in the middle.

Law of Common Region

Elements sharing a bounded area read as a group — used to cluster related content.

Design Changes

Design changes implemented for this cycle:

  • 1 Put the primary CTA above the fold and near the end (Serial Position Effect)
  • 2 Define what QWP is using keywords people search for (robo-advisor, managed investing, etc.)
  • 3 Explain the most important features on the landing page
  • 5 Description of the fees structure/pricing
  • 10 Account types at a glance — goal-oriented copy
  • 14 Popular portfolios and historical performance

UI work and Handoff

After UX wireframes, UI work starts — component specs (if not in the design system), padding for accessibility, font sizes for visual hierarchy, etc.

  • 1. Describing specs and behaviour of new components to devs
